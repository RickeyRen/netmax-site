Privacy Policy

NETMAX · Updated October 2026

No account, no NETMAX server

NETMAX has no account system and no server of its own. The developer does not receive your test results, addresses or history.

What stays on this device

History, settings, saved devices, monitors and presets are stored on your device. They leave it only when you export or share them, or when you turn on folder sync to a folder you choose.

What a test sends, and to whom

When you run a tool, your device contacts the services that tool needs, directly. Each of them sees your public IP address and the request itself: websites and devices you enter; IP lookup services (IPWHOIS.io, GeoJS, ipify and the regional internet registries); Cloudflare, Akamai, Google, Quad9, AliDNS and DNSPod for exit, DNS and STUN checks; Apple's time server; official service status pages; Claude and ChatGPT for the AI checkup; and Globalping probes when you use Global Ping. Their own privacy policies apply.

No tracking

NETMAX contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code, does not request location permission and does not read your Wi-Fi network name. Approximate locations shown in results come from public IP lookups.

Sharing

Share images and exports are created on your device. Addresses, cities and network operators are hidden by default; you choose whether to show them.

Children

NETMAX is a network diagnostics tool and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

This policy is updated when the app's data use changes. Questions: Support.

NETMAX is not affiliated with OpenAI or Anthropic. ChatGPT and Claude are trademarks of their owners.

隐私政策

NETMAX · 更新于 2026 年 10 月

无账户，无 NETMAX 服务器

NETMAX 没有账户系统，也没有自己的服务器。开发者不会收到你的测试结果、地址或历史记录。

保存在本机的内容

历史记录、设置、保存的设备、监控和预设都保存在你的设备上。只有在你导出或分享，或开启同步到你所选文件夹时，它们才会离开设备。

测试会发送什么、发给谁

当你运行工具时，设备会直接连接该工具所需的服务。每个服务都会看到你的公网 IP 地址和请求本身：你输入的网站和设备；IP 查询服务（IPWHOIS.io、GeoJS、ipify 及各区域互联网注册机构）；用于出口、DNS 和 STUN 检测的 Cloudflare、Akamai、Google、Quad9、阿里 DNS 和 DNSPod；Apple 时间服务器；官方服务状态页；AI 体检时的 Claude 与 ChatGPT；以及使用全球 Ping 时的 Globalping 探针。这些服务各自的隐私政策适用。

不追踪

NETMAX 不含广告、分析或追踪代码，不请求定位权限，也不读取 Wi-Fi 名称。结果中显示的大致位置来自公开的 IP 查询。

分享

分享图片和导出文件在设备上生成。地址、城市和运营商默认隐藏，是否显示由你决定。

儿童

NETMAX 是网络诊断工具，并非面向儿童。

变更与联系方式

当 App 的数据使用方式变化时，本政策会随之更新。如有疑问，请见技术支持。

NETMAX 与 OpenAI、Anthropic 无任何关联。ChatGPT 与 Claude 是其所有者的商标。
